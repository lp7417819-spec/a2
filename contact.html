Frequently Asked Inquiries

Salon & Acquisition FAQ

Key information regarding our timepiece allocations, private salon viewings, and international vault delivery.

Do I need an advance appointment to visit the Mercer Street Salon? +
While we warmly welcome drop-in patrons during standard salon hours, we highly recommend scheduling an appointment in advance to ensure a dedicated senior master horologist is available to guide your consultation and provide high-magnification stereomicroscope evaluations.
How are annual production allocations managed for tourbillon models? +
Due to the extreme labor required for hand-anglage and multi-axis regulation, our workshop produces fewer than fifty orbital tourbillon calibers annually worldwide. Allocations are confirmed in chronological order of deposit verification through our concierge desk.
What is your international delivery and vault courier policy? +
All acquisitions include complimentary fully insured, temperature-stabilized armored courier delivery across North America, the United Kingdom, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Every watch arrives in our signature handcrafted walnut winding box with COSC certificate, magnifying loupe, and titanium warranty card.
