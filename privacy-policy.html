Client Discretion Protocol

Privacy & Client Registry Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Orbit Dial Crest Atelier, Manhattan Salon

1. Introduction & Horological Commitment

At Orbit Dial Crest, operating from our digital horological domain and our flagship Manhattan salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, safeguarding the personal records and private collection archives of our patrons represents a fundamental commitment. We handle all collector identities, acquisition histories, and bespoke movement commissions with absolute discretion, deploying institutional-grade cryptographic protocols across our physical and digital systems. This Privacy Policy details the exact nature of records collected, how our systems protect your data, and the strict confidentiality standards governing our international salon network.

2. Scope of Collected Collector Records

When you navigate our online timepiece showcase, schedule a private viewing at our SoHo salon, request a bespoke tourbillon allocation, or register a serialized chronometer in our Geneva archives, we collect identifiable information including your full name, telephone contact, email address, physical delivery residence, and mechanical complication preferences. We do not harvest extraneous biometric or background data without explicit prior authorization, nor do we ever monetize, lease, or distribute our private client registry to third-party commercial brokers under any circumstances. Every record acquired is utilized exclusively to provide bespoke horological concierge assistance.

3. Purpose & Methods of Information Processing

The personal records gathered by Orbit Dial Crest serve strictly legitimate and transparent operational purposes designed to support your timepiece ownership experience. Specifically, your details enable our master watchmakers to prepare official COSC chronometer certificates, coordinate climate-stabilized armored courier transit, confirm private viewing reservations, and communicate critical technical bulletins regarding movement maintenance schedules. Furthermore, anonymized telemetry metrics assist our software engineers in optimizing interface responsiveness, refining interactive caliber calculation tools, and guaranteeing that our horological masterclasses comply with international data security standards.

4. Cryptographic Storage & Physical Salon Safeguards

We deploy enterprise-grade cryptographic encryption, isolated vault servers, and multi-factor authentication protocols to protect your confidential information from unauthorized access, interception, or disclosure. All digital correspondence submitted via our online concierge forms is encrypted utilizing TLS 1.3 cryptographic handshakes, while physical acquisition records at our Mercer Street salon are preserved within biometric-controlled archive vaults. Our internal staff undergoes rigorous annual data ethics training, ensuring that only certified horology advisors with verified operational need are permitted administrative access to client files.

5. Third-Party Logistics & Armored Transit Disclosures

Orbit Dial Crest does not disclose private client records to external commercial marketing agencies or unrelated corporate third parties. Disclosures are strictly limited to vetted specialized logistics partners, including licensed fine-art armored couriers, secure payment clearing networks, and official Swiss chronometer testing authorities when mandated for certification. All external logistics partners operate under binding non-disclosure covenants and are legally prohibited from retaining, distributing, or utilizing your personal data for any purpose beyond the direct delivery of your acquired timepiece.

6. Your Statutory Rights & Data Governance

Regardless of your jurisdiction of residence, Orbit Dial Crest upholds your comprehensive legal rights regarding your personal records under applicable international privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA frameworks. You maintain the absolute right to request formal verification of stored data, demand immediate correction of administrative errors, request complete electronic erasure of client profiles, or restrict specific analytical processing at any time. To exercise any of these privacy privileges or submit a formal compliance request, you may contact our dedicated Data Protection Officer directly via our official concierge phone line.

7. Atelier Contact & Legal Inquiries

If you have questions, formal grievances, or clarification requests regarding our data governance protocols, please direct your correspondence to our official atelier headquarters. You may reach our compliance department by writing directly to Orbit Dial Crest, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by calling our official telephone line at +1-888-777-5845 during standard salon operating hours. Our legal liaison will review your submission promptly and deliver a comprehensive formal written response within thirty business days of verified receipt.
