2. Mechanical Precision & Chronometric Tolerances

Mechanical timepieces, orbital tourbillons, and astronomical perpetual calendars are micro-mechanical instruments regulated to certified chronometer tolerances (such as official COSC standards of -2/+2 seconds per day). Daily chronometric rates can vary naturally based on individual wearing habits, gravitational orientation, atmospheric barometric pressure, ambient temperature swings, and magnetic field exposure. Patrons are strongly advised to adhere strictly to our official operation manual, avoiding manual calendar adjustments during the mechanical gear change window between 8:00 PM and 3:00 AM to prevent damage to internal levers.

3. Meteorite Dials & Crystalline Variations

Natural iron-nickel meteorites utilized in our signature dial series are extraterrestrial geological specimens featuring organic Widmanstätten crystal growth patterns formed over millions of years of celestial cooling. These distinct geometric striations, microscopic inclusions, and surface textures are inherent hallmarks of authentic cosmic origin rather than manufacturing defects or material flaws. Orbit Dial Crest disclaims liability for natural visual variations between individual dial cuts, as every finished meteorite dial possesses an entirely unique, non-repeatable crystalline fingerprint.

4. Secondary Market & Investment Commentary

Although select high-complication mechanical timepieces historically retain or appreciate in value among international collectors, past auction realizations cannot guarantee future secondary market appraisals. Patrons should base acquisition decisions upon personal aesthetic appreciation, micro-mechanical craftsmanship quality, and utility rather than speculative financial expectations.
